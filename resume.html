08 / Professional direction

I want to use AI to make human work easier, not more complicated.

My interest in AI is not limited to financial ROI or replacing manual effort. I am interested in systems that reduce friction, preserve human judgment, make complex information easier to use, and give people better control over decisions.

I separate AI-generated output from AI-powered systems. A generated answer may be useful. An AI-powered application also needs reliable data, clear logic, security, governance, evaluation, and a human decision boundary.

The goal is not to add AI everywhere. The goal is to make useful work easier.
