13 / AI philosophy

AI-generated output is not the same as an AI-powered system

The difference is the engineering and accountability around the model.

AI-generated

A useful artifact

  • Fast draft or answer
  • May be polished
  • Can still be unsupported
  • Does not prove workflow reliability

AI-powered

An accountable system

  • Reliable data and definitions
  • Versioned logic and permissions
  • Evaluation and failure handling
  • Human decision boundary

The goal is not to add AI everywhere. The goal is to make useful work easier.
