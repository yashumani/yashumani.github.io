## Video Overview prompt

Create a polished narrated Video Overview for a technical portfolio case study. The audience is AI/ML leaders, technical recruiters, and engineering reviewers. Explain the project as a production-grade research pipeline, not a chatbot demo.

Focus on this story:

1. The problem: one-shot AI research answers can hallucinate and lack a clear audit trail.
2. The solution: this runtime converts research into a source-grounded pipeline.
3. The architecture: source adapters, extraction, evidence graph, hybrid retrieval, knowledge compiler, and critique/repair.
4. The proof: 13 connectors, 92 commits, 200+ tests, Temporal orchestration, DSPy typed programs, Promptfoo gates, MLflow tracking, and Azure deployment.
5. The takeaway: every published claim should be traceable to evidence.

Style requirements:

- Duration: 60 to 120 seconds.
- Visual style: clean technical explainer, modern architecture diagrams, subtle motion, no gimmicks.
- Tone: credible, precise, confident, low-hype.
- Avoid: generic AI buzzwords, cartoon robots, fake dashboards, private employer references.
- End frame: "Agentic Knowledge & Research Runtime — source-grounded AI research pipeline."

## Short vertical video prompt

Create a 60-second vertical explainer for LinkedIn or a portfolio preview.

Hook: "What if an AI research assistant had to prove every claim before publishing?"

Storyboard:

- Frame 1: scattered sources entering the system.
- Frame 2: extraction turns raw material into chunks, tables, and media.
- Frame 3: evidence graph links claims to sources.
- Frame 4: hybrid retrieval finds support using keyword, vector, and reranking.
- Frame 5: critique and repair catches unsupported claims.
- Frame 6: final cited artifact with audit trail.

Use on-screen metric callouts: 13 connectors, 200+ tests, 92 commits, Temporal, MLflow, Promptfoo.
