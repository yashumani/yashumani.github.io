Definitions for Decision-Grade Analytics

A practical agreement for the meaning and behavior behind a reported number.

PublishedAugust 12, 2026Last reviewedAugust 13, 2026StatusWorking paper

01Why I wrote this

Two reports can use the same label and still disagree because the formula, time window, exclusions, or comparison changed.

02What needs to be agreed

  • The business question.
  • The formula and sign.
  • The lowest valid level of detail.
  • The date and period rules.
  • The valid rollups.
  • The comparison version.
  • The owner and source path.

03Where I land

A dashboard can display a number. It cannot settle what the number means. The definition has to exist before the chart.
