02 / Impact model

AI-powered systems, not AI-generated demos

AI-generated means a model produced an artifact. AI-powered means AI is one component inside an accountable system with defined inputs, outputs, owners, tests, metrics, and audit trails.

The systems here are positioned as the second kind: governed pipelines where value is proven through traceability, reproducibility, evaluation gates, and deployment discipline — not output quality alone.
