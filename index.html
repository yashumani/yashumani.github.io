I'm Yashu Sharma, a data scientist and AI engineer based in New Jersey. I design and build production-grade AI systems — the kind with real deployments, automated tests, and evidence-gated release discipline, not weekend prototypes.

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and systems engineering: agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented research pipelines, and automated MLOps platforms. I care about systems that hold up under real use, not just in a demo.

I built the Agentic Knowledge & Research Runtime, a multi-agent platform that turns scattered source material — web pages, video, code, research feeds — into cited, critique-reviewed research artifacts. I also built the Automated ML Pipeline Platform, an Azure ML system that runs a model tournament to automatically find, tune, and register the best model for a given dataset — including a direct application to telecom churn prediction.

I enjoy learning about hard technical problems and working with people who care about doing things properly. Reach out if you want to talk shop or find a way to work together.
