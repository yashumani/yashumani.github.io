Sources and transparency

One primary reading edition; multiple interpretive lenses

Open source notes and study commitments

The primary reading edition for sequence and numbering is Bhagavad Gita by Sri Swami Sivananda, published by The Divine Life Society. That edition describes the Gita as containing 701 Sanskrit verses. Many other editions use 700; this journey identifies numbering differences instead of silently forcing them into one scheme.

Translations and commentaries are interpretations, not substitutes for the Sanskrit text. Where an issue is disputed, the lesson will identify the disagreement and compare more than one serious perspective.
